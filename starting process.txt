================================================================================
BANK ACCOUNT — WHICH TYPE & WHERE NEEDED
================================================================================


OPTION A: Use Your Personal Savings Account (Simplest to start)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    • You CAN use your existing savings account for receiving payments
    • Google AdSense, affiliates, etc. will pay into it
    • Works fine until you cross ₹20 lakh/year revenue
    • Downside: Business and personal money gets mixed


OPTION B: Open a Current Account in Business Name (Recommended)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    • Open after getting GST registration or Udyam registration
    • Keeps business money separate from personal
    • Needed for: Professional image, GST filing, scaling up
    • Banks: HDFC, ICICI, Kotak, RazorpayX (zero balance options available)

    Documents needed to open current account:
    • PAN Card
    • Aadhaar
    • GST Certificate OR Udyam Registration Certificate
    • Business address proof
    • 2 passport photos


WHERE YOUR BANK ACCOUNT DETAILS ARE NEEDED:
───────────────────────────────────────────
    You'll enter bank details (Account No, IFSC, Account holder name) at:

    1. Google AdSense (ad revenue payment)
    2. Affiliate programs (commission payment)
    3. Hosting providers (for any refunds)
    4. Payment gateways like Razorpay/Stripe (if selling anything later)
    5. GST portal (for refunds if applicable)
    6. Sponsored content payments (brands pay via bank transfer)

    Payment methods from foreign companies:
    • Google AdSense → Direct bank transfer (INR) via EFT
    • Amazon Associates (US) → Direct deposit or cheque
    • BetterHelp affiliates → PayPal or bank wire (USD)
    • Other affiliates → PayPal, Payoneer, or direct bank transfer

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 8: GOOGLE ADSENSE (Start earning from ads)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    WHAT:   Google places ads on your site, pays you
    WHERE:  www.google.com/adsense
    COST:   FREE to join

    ⚠️  APPLY ONLY AFTER: You have 15-25 quality blog posts published

    Information needed to register:
    • Google account
    • Your website URL
    • Your name (as on bank account)
    • Address (for PIN verification — they mail a physical letter!)
    • Phone number

    Information needed for PAYMENT setup (after approval):
    • Bank account holder name (EXACTLY as on bank)
    • Bank account number
    • IFSC code
    • Bank name and branch

    Approval process:
    1. Apply with your website
    2. Google reviews (takes 2-14 days)
    3. If approved: Add ad code to website
    4. If rejected: They tell you why (fix issues, reapply in 2 weeks)
    5. After earning $10: Google mails a PIN to your physical address
    6. Enter the PIN to verify address
    7. Payments start once balance crosses $100 (₹~8,000)

    Payment cycle: Earnings by Month end → Paid by 21st of next month
    Payment method: Direct bank transfer (EFT to Indian bank account)

    Common rejection reasons:
    • Not enough content (need 15-20 quality posts minimum)
    • Content too thin (short articles)
    • Site looks unfinished (no about page, privacy policy, contact page)
    • Navigation issues
    • Policy violation (explicit content — keep it educational!)

STEP 15: GOVERNMENT/TAX REGISTRATIONS (India)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────


A) UDYAM REGISTRATION (MSME — Micro, Small & Medium Enterprise)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    WHERE:  udyamregistration.gov.in
    COST:   FREE
    TIME:   Instant (online, Aadhaar-based)

    Information needed:
    • Aadhaar number (linked to mobile)
    • PAN number
    • Business name (your blog/brand name)
    • Type of enterprise: Service
    • Business activity: "Online media publishing" or "Digital content services"
    • Bank account details
    • Business address
    • Investment in equipment (your computer, ₹30,000-1,00,000)
    • Number of employees

    WHY: Gives you MSME certificate → helps open current account,
         get government schemes, looks professional.


B) GST REGISTRATION
────────────────────
    WHERE:  www.gst.gov.in
    COST:   FREE
    WHEN:   Mandatory when revenue crosses ₹20 lakh/year
             (Recommended earlier for professionalism)

    Information needed:
    • PAN card
    • Aadhaar card
    • Business address proof (electricity bill/rent agreement)
    • Bank account statement or cancelled cheque
    • Photograph
    • Digital signature (Class 2 DSC) — costs ₹500–1000
    • Constitution of business: Proprietorship
    • Business name
    • Nature of business: "Information Technology and Computer Services"
      or "Other service activities" → "Web portal operations"

    What it gives you:
    • GSTIN number (needed for professional invoices)
    • Ability to claim input tax credit on expenses
    • Required for current account in some banks
    • Shows legitimacy to sponsors/advertisers

    Note on GST for blog income:
    • Ad income from Google (foreign company) = Export of services = 0% GST
    • But you still file NIL returns monthly/quarterly
    • Indian affiliate income = 18% GST applicable
    • Get a CA to handle this — don't DIY GST


C) PAN (Permanent Account Number) — You likely already have this
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    If not:
    WHERE:  www.onlineservices.nsdl.com/paam/endUserRegisterContact.html
    COST:   ₹107
    
    Needed for: Everything (AdSense, affiliates, GST, bank, tax filing)

THINGS PEOPLE OFTEN FORGET
================================================================================

    1. AdSense PIN VERIFICATION — Google sends a physical letter to your
       home address with a PIN. If you don't enter it, you don't get paid.
       Arrives in 2-4 weeks. Keep your correct address in AdSense.

    2. TAX on foreign income — Google pays you from Ireland/Singapore.
       This is foreign income. You must declare it in ITR. No TDS is cut
       by Google, so YOU must pay advance tax quarterly or face interest.

    3. KEEP RECORDS — Save every invoice, receipt, payment screenshot.
       All business expenses are tax-deductible (hosting, tools, writer
       payments, laptop, internet bill — everything used for the blog).

    4. DOMAIN AUTO-RENEWAL — Turn it ON. If your domain expires and
       someone else buys it, you lose everything. Set auto-renew.

    5. TWO-FACTOR AUTH (2FA) — Enable 2FA on EVERY account, especially:
       AdSense, bank, email, hosting, domain registrar.
       One hacked account = entire business at risk.

    6. BACKUP EMAIL — Keep a backup recovery email. If you lose access
       to your primary email, you lose access to everything.

    7. DOCUMENT EVERYTHING — Keep a master spreadsheet with:
       - All account usernames/emails
       - What payment method is linked where
       - Renewal dates for domain, hosting, tools
       - Login details (use a password manager like Bitwarden — free)


================================================================================
        BLOG SYSTEM — OWNER'S VIEW (How the whole thing works, simply)
================================================================================


There are 3 SIDES to this system:

  A. YOUR SIDE (You / Your Writers)  — where blogs are created
  B. THE SYSTEM (Server / Machine)   — where blogs are stored & managed
  C. READER'S SIDE (Public Website)  — where people read & you earn money

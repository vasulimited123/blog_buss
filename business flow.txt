================================================================================
THINGS YOU MIGHT BE MISSING — Important Business Considerations
================================================================================


1. LEGAL & COMPLIANCE (Very important for health/wellness niche)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• GST Registration — Required once revenue crosses ₹20 lakh/year
• Medical Disclaimer — Every page needs "this is not medical advice"
• Privacy Policy + Cookie Consent — Required for AdSense & EU visitors
• FSSAI (if recommending supplements) — May need disclosure
• Income Tax — Blog income is taxable business income
• CA/Accountant fees — ₹2,000–5,000/month


2. CONTENT QUALITY & RISK
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Plagiarism checker — ₹1,000–3,000/month (Copyscape, Grammarly)
• Fact-checking process — Misinformation in health niche = Google penalty + legal risk
• Content insurance (optional) — Protects against lawsuits from bad health advice


3. BACKUP & DISASTER RECOVERY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Daily database backups — Automated, ₹500–1,000/month
• Version control — If site goes down, can you restore it?
• Secondary domain — In case your primary gets penalized by Google


4. COMPETITION & BRAND
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Competitor analysis tools — Know what similar sites are doing
• Brand protection — Trademark your brand name (₹5,000–10,000 one-time)
• Multiple language (future) — Hindi versions can double your audience


5. CASH RESERVE (Critical!)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Keep 3-6 months of expenses in reserve
• Phase 1: ₹1,00,000–3,00,000 reserve
• Phase 2: ₹3,00,000–6,00,000 reserve
• You WILL lose money for 6-12 months before profit


6. YOUR TIME COST
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Even if you don't pay yourself, your time has value
• First 6 months: expect 4-6 hours/day managing this business
• Account for opportunity cost of what else you could be doing

About Dr. Agrawal

Dr. R.S. Agrawal is a respected Homoeopathic physician with more than 25 years of clinical experience in caring for patients and guiding them toward lasting wellbeing. As Principal of Narayan Shree Homoeopathy College, he has dedicated much of his career not only to healing, but to teaching and shaping the next generation of homoeopathic doctors.

Over the decades, Dr. Agrawal has treated a wide spectrum of patients, earning a reputation for patience, careful listening, and a deeply human approach to care. He believes that healing is never only physical — that true wellbeing includes the mind, the emotions, and the relationships that give life meaning.

It is this philosophy that led him to found Elevate Life: a space where honest, compassionate, judgment-free guidance on health, relationships, and emotional wellbeing is made available to everyone.
